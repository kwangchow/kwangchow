## Hi, I'm Sean 👋

I help games and entertainment businesses decide what to build on new platforms, then turn promising ideas into playable prototypes and pilots.
Thirty years making and shipping games. I worked on the original Grand Theft Auto, produced the BAFTA-winning Quarrel and established InGAME, the UK's national games R&D centre.

Proof of work:

- **[InGAME](https://seantaylor.work/ingame-impact)**. Established and led the UK's £5.3m national games R&D centre: 177 projects and a forecast £84.7m for Scotland's economy (£15.90 per £1 of public funding).

- **[TV as a Games Platform](tab:https://seantaylor.work/tv-as-a-games-platform.html)**. Casual games before the casual boom. Agile before it had a manifesto. An app store before the App Store. 
How a small studio in Scotland shipped more than 150 games on a set-top box built to show television, not play games.

- **[Quarrel - Award-Winning Original IP](https://seantaylor.work/quarrel)**. I was Producer of Denki's word-strategy game from vertical slice to release on Xbox 360 and iOS, winning BAFTA Scotland Best Game.

- **[Punk Provocations](https://seantaylor.work/punk-provocations/)**. A 2026 remix of Punk Playthings: Provocations for 21st Century Game Makers, the book I co-wrote with Chris Lowthorpe telling game makers to be more punk.

- **[After the Games Industry](https://seantaylor.work/amusement-economy.html)**. 15 theses on the amusement economy and the end of the industrial production line..
